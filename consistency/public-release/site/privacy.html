Privacy

The showcase processes pasted and local-file model contents in your browser. It does not upload those contents, add analytics, or store your inputs in browser persistence. Reloading discards your inputs.

GitHub Pages serves this website and its static assets, and receives the normal web requests needed to load them. Following an external link takes you to that provider's site. See GitHub's privacy statement for its hosting policy.
