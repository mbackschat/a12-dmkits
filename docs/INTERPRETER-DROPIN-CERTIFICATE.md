# Interpreter drop-in COMPATIBILITY CERTIFICATE — the eval-surface gate for retiring `--kernel` eval

> **What this is.** The documented removal gate that [`INTERPRETER-SPEC.md`](INTERPRETER-SPEC.md) §8a makes the precondition for retiring the `--kernel` eval flag. It enumerates every capability the kernel's `--kernel` **evaluation** path provides, states the interpreter's parity status for each, names the **exact lock** that proves it, records the **residuals**, argues **why the differential estate spans the surface** (0-divergences-on-a-corpus is not proof-on-all-inputs), and renders a **verdict**.
>
> **Scope — EVALUATION only ("Scope A").** This certifies the kernel's *evaluation* role: `model eval` / `rule eval` / `model compute` and the JVM `--kernel` behaviour behind them. It does **NOT** certify the kernel's *consistency / MVK-acceptance* role (`rule check` / `model check` / `checkConsistency` / the F4 "the real kernel accepts this" guarantee) — that stays kernel-backed ("Scope B", a separate deferred question). So even after `--kernel` eval is retired, dmtool keeps bundling the kernel as the MVK consistency oracle and the `:adapter` differential oracle. Never collapse the two roles.
>
> **This document was the removal gate; retirement has since EXECUTED (Set V, 2026-07-12).** Building the certificate was an audit + documentation deliverable that gated retirement on the then-owner-ratified green-or-documented-equivalent verdict and changed eval behavior only where the audit surfaced a real bug. A 2026-07-19 source-and-evidence re-audit found additional legal-input, platform, mutation, and coverage dimensions outside that original certificate. The historical retirement decision remains recorded; this document must no longer be read as a current strict whole-domain certificate.

Record: [`INTERPRETER-FINDINGS.md`](INTERPRETER-FINDINGS.md) IF122 (executed retirement), IF134 (NFR-commit audit), and IF135 (follow-on source/certificate audit). The complete live set is [INTERPRETER-GAPS.md](INTERPRETER-GAPS.md); certificate-relevant examples include the commonMain raw-assembly boundary (IG68) and completed included-reference preparation (IF146), Europe/Berlin full history + the enforced supported-zone scope (IF155; wider zone-id domain = IG83 Route A), completed JS decimal and regex host semantics (IF163/IF164), charset sequences (IF151), the complete custom-validator callback/result contract (IF150 + IF152), RNU boolean composition (IF149), computation tolerance (IF148) and bare-group compute quantifier order (IF147), the now-closed lossy unsupported handling (IF139), the now-fixed mutable-document caches (IF142), and the completed derived evidence matrix (IF140). Related: [`KERNEL-DECOUPLING-PROPOSAL.md`](KERNEL-DECOUPLING-PROPOSAL.md) (Stage 2 = model assembly) and [`KERNEL-SEMANTICS.md`](KERNEL-SEMANTICS.md).

## 2. The coverage argument — why the differential estate SPANS the surface

"0 divergences on the corpus" is **not** "proof on all inputs." The argument that the estate spans the eval surface rests on five independent, always-current mechanisms — three *breadth* nets and two *combination* nets — plus an explicit hole-hunt.

**(1) Every construct is handled — a derived guard, not a whole-surface certificate.** `CatalogParityTest` reads the live `operators.json` universe, the live `PredicateOp`/`ConstantOp`/`FunctionOp` enums (what the engine handles), and the fuzz net, and build-fails if any catalog construct is unclassified or any handled callable is un-fuzzed-without-a-reason. Current: **110/110 handled, 0 deferred, 0 excluded.** `CapabilityEvidenceMatrixTest` (IF140) derives one strategy-evidence cell per live operator and independently enumerates the formal, computation, configuration, customization, platform, and assembly axes: **110 live operator tri-checks, 19 non-operator tri-checks, 6 explicit reasoned exclusions, 135/135 cells accounted.** That proves the declared evidence inventory and keeps exclusions visible; it does not turn the open legal-input gaps below into covered behavior.

**(2) Every handled construct has compile-checked strategy evidence.** The 2026-07-12 name sweep remains historical breadth evidence, but IF140 replaces its rot-prone operator claim with `CatalogOperatorStrategyDiffTest`: ordinary valid catalog examples are injected into a live model and evaluated by the interpreter, Groovy-dynamic, and generated Java; the four specialist/computation-only cells point to their dedicated tri-checks. Non-operator evidence also names real test classes by `KClass`, so rename/delete breaks compilation, and independent-universe guards fail on an omitted, new-unclassified, duplicated, or axis-less cell. The matrix records CI/JVM, CI/JVM+JS portable replay, and opt-in/JVM real-corpus tiers and generates its certificate summary from those cells.

**(3) The renderer-modifier net closes the class the operator guards structurally cannot see.** `CatalogParityTest.fuzzNetCoversRendererModifiers` keys on what `DslRenderer` can EMIT (`$`/`*`/`Having`) rather than the operator inventory — the exact net whose absence let the IF47 `$`-correlation gap hide (a modifier is not an operator, so no operator-keyed guard covers it).

**(4) Two combination fuzz nets sweep what no hand-picked case reaches.** `InterpreterKernelFuzzTest` (codes multiset, tri-differential: `interp == groovy == java-static` — so it also gates the **static codegen** the runtime oracle can't see; on a legal-input split the ratified anchor is Groovy-dynamic and a static-Java divergence is recorded via the corpus `divergences` mechanism, never adopted — IF136) and its stronger sibling `FullMessageFuzzDiffTest` (`code|type|pointer` — trips on wrong polarity / wrong row); `ModelFuzzDiffTest` varies the *model* (random depth 2–4); `ComputationFuzzDiffTest` sweeps compute chains; `SiblingGroupFuzzDiffTest` the sibling-group shape; `PartialValidationFuzzDiffTest` the (document × relevant-subset) space. All CI-gated (`-PrunFuzz`), green this run.

**(5) Two real-corpus sweeps — kernel-team-authored rules over seeded instances, dynamic/interpreter throughout and generated-Java where generation succeeds, both seeding modes.** `RealCorpusSweepTest` covers the A12 Tools workspaces (107 models, 690 rules, 646 computations) + `a12-kernel-mm` (15 canonical metamodels): a genuine divergence between engines that evaluate fails the sweep; generated-Java rejections are counted and skipped rather than converted into evidence. The sweep is opt-in and prepares each model with the kernel before the interpreter sees it, so it is breadth evidence—not an always-on tri-engine/assembly certificate.

  - **nice mode:** `RealCorpusSweepTest -PrunCorpusSweep -Da12.corpus1.dir=<workspaces>` → JUnit XML **`skipped="0"`, 0 divergences** (workspaces 107 models / 530 validateFull + 350 compute; kernel-mm 15 models). The only skips: **5+5 kernel-codegen rejections** (the kernel itself refuses the input) — not an interpreter gap.
  - **hostile mode:** `+ -Dcorpus.hostile=true` (~every fourth NUMBER cell violates an operand-invalidity class) → JUnit XML **`tests="2" skipped="0" failures="0" errors="0"`, 0 divergences** (this set's run — §0).

  The `skipped="0"` matters: it proves both corpora were *present and actually swept*, not `assumeTrue`-skipped into a false green.

  **A precision on what the sweep proves.** The sweep pipeline prepares each model with `CorpusModelPreparer`, which uses the **kernel's** `expand` to produce a self-contained model *before* handing it to the interpreter. So the sweep exercises the interpreter's **evaluation** surface on already-assembled models — it does **not** exercise the interpreter's own assembly layer (Stage 2). Assembly parity is carried separately by `ModelIndexParityTest` and the focused production-preparer locks. In particular, `IncludeAssemblyStrategyDiffTest` independently prepares the kernel and interpreter legs, then tri-checks the mounted model through dynamic-Groovy, generated Java, and the interpreter; the core/CLI/JVM+Node include guards cover the reference slots, lifecycle, and refusal boundary that the corpus sweep cannot.

**The hole-hunt (the honest part).** With breadth (1–3) and combination (4–5) established, the audit asked: *is there any capability / operator / formal check / message locale / customization point that NO lock exercises?* Findings:

- **No un-locked operator, formal check, or quantifier** — every one maps to a DiffTest (§1, audit (2)).
- **Message locales:** the interpolation mechanism is a per-locale template map lookup (locale-agnostic by construction); EN + DE are differentially locked; a third locale is the same mechanism, different key — not a hole.
- **Customization/configuration points:** the six input axes in §1d are mapped. The remaining named semantic domain is the wider non-Berlin timezone-id domain (IG83 Route A, after IF155 shipped Berlin full history + the enforced supported scope). JS decimal and regex host semantics shipped as IF163/IF164; combined-grapheme charset entries shipped as IF151; custom-validator code identity shipped as IF150 and its context/cardinality/message/consumer-contract completion as IF152.
- **The original hole-hunt surfaced R1 and R5; it did not exhaust the legal domain.** R1 is now fully closed by IF146's reference-aware preparation and tri-engine include lock. R5 is now NARROWED + ENFORCED (owner Route-B decision): IF155 closed Berlin's full history and the supported-scope refusal, leaving only the wider zone-id domain as a documented limit (IG83 Route A). ## 4. The verdict

**Historical retirement verdict: GREEN-OR-DOCUMENTED-EQUIVALENT (owner-ratified 2026-07-12). Current audit verdict: strong evidence on the exercised JVM surface, not a current strict drop-in certificate.** `--kernel` retirement remains executed, but the follow-on audit found additional open exactness/capability dimensions. The derived matrix is now complete (IF140); its explicit exclusions and the authoritative current list in [INTERPRETER-GAPS.md](INTERPRETER-GAPS.md) prevent that accounting from being mistaken for a whole-domain verdict.

The eval-core has broad, valuable evidence: 110/110 catalog constructs are handled; the capability matrix derives generated-Java evidence or an explicit reasoned exclusion per declared cell; renderer-modifier coverage, combination fuzzing, and prepared-model corpus sweeps agree on the cases they execute. The limits remain explicit: corpora do not exercise raw assembly or every identified platform/configuration corner, and several former “documented equivalents” are observable exactness gaps rather than mere output projections.

**G1 and G1′ are closed.** IF146 replaces the narrow Set-U transform with reference-aware preparation for every rule/computation fragment, include level, nesting depth, short-name target, and legal `$`/`Having` form. `IncludeAssemblyStrategyDiffTest` compares kernel expansion and both kernel strategies with the interpreter; focused core/CLI/JVM+Node guards cover the syntax, lifecycle, and genuine-unmapped refusal siblings. The remaining highlighted state is:
- **G2 (R5)—timezone.** Europe/Berlin's COMPLETE history is now differentially locked (IF155, superseding IF128's modern-only lock), and the supported zone set `{UTC, GMT, Europe/Berlin}` is enforced with a corrective refusal for any other kernel-legal zone. The kernel's wider accepted zone-id domain is the deferred Route A of [IG83](INTERPRETER-GAPS.md) — a documented supported-scope limit, not full kernel timezone parity.

`--kernel` retirement is executed (Set V; IF122); the kernel remains the bundled MVK oracle and `:adapter` differential oracle. IF140's derived evidence matrix is complete. Re-certification still requires the remaining live correctness gaps to close or become explicit owner-ratified/enforced input-contract exclusions. Until then, cite the named locks and their exercised domains—not a whole-domain GREEN label.
