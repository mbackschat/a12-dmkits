# Portable consistency checker — gaps

> **What this is** — where the kernel-free `:consistency` checker ([CONSISTENCY-API](CONSISTENCY-API.md)) reports a model differently from A12's `checkConsistency`: a class it leaves unnamed, or one it names where A12 names another. Each row gives the input, A12's measured class, the kernel law that measured it, and the plan item that settles it. Ids are `DG<n>`, drawn from the chunks declared once in [DEV-API-GAPS](DEV-API-GAPS.md)'s `dg-id-chunks` line, which serves both ledgers.
>
> **Lifecycle.** A gap lives here only while open. When it ships, its row is DELETED in the same commit: the measured behaviour already lives in its [KERNEL-FINDINGS](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md) entry, and the sweep ledger turns the divergence into an agreement. No shipped list, ever.
>
> **Current state.** Since 2026-09-26 the checker's divergences are discovered by the near-miss sweep, and its committed ledger (`adapter/src/test/resources/checker-near-miss/clusters.tsv`) is their list. A row found by hand names its input, the missing structure behind it and the stage rebuild in [CONFORMANCE-PLAN](CONFORMANCE-PLAN.md) that settles it, and is kept here until it becomes a sweep seed.

## 1. Open

- **DG130 — three length readers can judge a constant against a length A12 does not treat as a bound** (found 2026-10-13, beside the hint-list fix). `IndexKeyConstant.stringAdmits` takes any positive `minLength`/`maxLength`, and `FieldValueOperatorOperands.conversionDefect` and `DateConstructionSlots` read the declared `maxLength` as it stands. A12 judges nothing with a member outside 1 to 99,999 ([KF556](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf556), measured for the type's own laws and its hint list), so beside such a member the checker may add one class to a model A12 already refuses with the range sentence; it cannot refuse a model A12 takes. Settles with one agreement row per reader, then `lengthInRange` at each that differs.
- **DG133 — four enum families may name a supplementary-bearing entry** (found 2026-10-13, cold review). The rule that no coded finding names an entry carrying a supplementary-plane character holds in `EnumCategories` and `EnumValueLabels.duplicateTokenFindings` only; `EnumTextForbiddenCharacters` (`MVK_INTERNAL_VALUE_INVALID`, `MVK_CATEGORY_VALUE_INVALID`, `MVK_DISPLAY_VALUE_INVALID`), `EnumSpecialValue` (`MVK_S_VALUE_INVALID`) and `EnumValueLabels` (`MVK_DISPLAY_VALUES_DUPLICATE`) do not apply it. Each would add a class to a model A12 already refuses. Settles with one row per family in `CheckerEnumDeclarationStreamAgreementTest`.
- **DG134 — the checker's hand-found codeless divergences live only in the plan** (found 2026-10-13, cold review). [CONFORMANCE-PLAN](CONFORMANCE-PLAN.md) M3-CODELESS lists refused `DateRangeType` pairs, a group name past 60 characters, two rule-name shapes and an empty `errorCode` under an `ONLY` filter as measured and open, while this ledger said none were. Settles when M3-CODELESS turns them into sweep seeds or fixes them.
- **DG135 — four smaller checker residuals** (found 2026-10-13, cold review). `RK_ENUM_TEXT_LINE_BREAK` and `RK_UNDECLARED_MESSAGE_LOCALE` are emitted but missing from [CONSISTENCY-API](CONSISTENCY-API.md), and no guard ties emitted codes to it; a TypeDef-typed body is skipped with no `CoverageGap`; an `xml`-prefixed ancestor beside an underscore child name is unmeasured, while `ElementNameAlphabet` picks one class; and the test-side `CheckerKernelVerdict` keys the 15 and 14 digit sentences whatever member raised them. Each settles on its own.
- **DG138 — the checker names `MVK_MIN_VALUE_BIGGER_THAN_MAX_VALUE` beside a printed bound A12's formal check refuses** (found 2026-10-14, Batch T). A12 withholds the class when a bound's printed form fails its codeless check: `-0` at scale 0 (1 over −0.4) and more than 15 digits (1e16 over 1; 1e15 over 1 at scale 1). It does not withhold for the value-limit rule alone (1e14 over 1 at scale 1) or for −0.04 at scale 1; measured through `dmtool model check`, not yet locked. The checker compares the bounds regardless ([KF569](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf569)), so it adds one class to a model A12 already refuses and cannot refuse one A12 takes. Settles with those rows in `CheckerDeclarationAdmissionAgreementTest` and the printed-bound check in [CONFORMANCE-PLAN](CONFORMANCE-PLAN.md) M3-CODELESS.

## 2. Out of scope

- **What a document's evaluation fires.** The checker judges a model statically; runtime behaviour is the interpreter's, and its divergences are [INTERPRETER-GAPS](INTERPRETER-GAPS.md)'.
