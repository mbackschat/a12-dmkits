# Portable consistency checker — gaps

> **What this is** — where the kernel-free `:consistency` checker ([CONSISTENCY-API](CONSISTENCY-API.md)) reports a model differently from A12's `checkConsistency`: a class it leaves unnamed, or one it names where A12 names another. Each row gives the input, A12's measured class, the kernel law that measured it, and the plan item that settles it. Ids are `DG<n>`, drawn from the chunks declared once in [DEV-API-GAPS](DEV-API-GAPS.md)'s `dg-id-chunks` line, which serves both ledgers.
>
> **Lifecycle.** A gap lives here only while open. When it ships, its row is DELETED in the same commit: the measured behaviour already lives in its [KERNEL-FINDINGS](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md) entry, and the sweep ledger turns the divergence into an agreement. No shipped list, ever.
>
> **Current state.** Since 2026-09-26 the checker's divergences are discovered by the near-miss sweep, and its committed ledger (`adapter/src/test/resources/checker-near-miss/clusters.tsv`) is their list. A row found by hand names its input, the missing structure behind it and the stage rebuild in [CONFORMANCE-PLAN](CONFORMANCE-PLAN.md) that settles it, and is kept here until it becomes a sweep seed.

## 1. Open

- **DG147 — a `$`-marked operand in a `Having` body is unjudged by most operand families (opened 2026-10-23).** A12 judges a marked reference with the gates an unmarked one meets, addressed by the enclosing rule's own iteration ([KF598](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf598)). Three families read the mark: the value operators (`CurrentRepetition`, `AbsValue`, `Length`, `FieldValueAsNumber`, `FieldValueAsString`), the group lists and the field-value read. The other families that read a call operand through `operandSpec` or `CallOperand.path` see no reference in a marked operand, so they stay silent and name no gap. The search was a `grep` of `consistency/src/commonMain` for `operandSpec(`, `operand.path` and `operandReferenceIn(` against `capturedPath`; it matched 40 files, and the families among them were not each read. Settled family by family: a kernel row on that family's carrier, then `capturedPath` with the rule's own addressing in the family, as `FieldValueOperatorOperands.kt` does.
- **DG146 — iteration-guard shapes the checker leaves to A12 (opened 2026-10-22, narrowed the same day when the law landed).** The `ITERATION_GUARD` family reports `MVK_NEG_CONDITION_IN_ITERATION` and `MVK_PARALLEL_ITERATION_INVALID` on the measured shapes ([KF537](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf537), [KF595](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf595), [KF596](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf596)). It declines, and names the rule in a coverage gap instead (`IterationGuardTest`), where the rule reports on a group or its condition uses a `$` marker, a selection keyed by a field, a constant selection that still iterates a level above it in a rule iterating in parallel, a leaf outside the measured set (a pattern, `RepetitionNotUnique`, a tolerance comparison, the field-and-type `Valid`/`Invalid`, a keyword comparison), a group operand in a field list other than `FieldValuesNotUnique`, or a root group read as a condition, where only a starred reference names the error field while another iterates, where a comparison uses `SumOfProducts`, or where a constant beyond the exact range of a double decides. It also declines a whole connective when any one part is undecided, even where A12's answer is already fixed by the others. Each of those is a silence A12 can break: A12 refuses a root-group condition and a group target the checker declines (`CheckerIterationGuardAgreementTest`). Settled shape by shape: a kernel row in that differential, then the leaf in `IterationGuard.kt`.
