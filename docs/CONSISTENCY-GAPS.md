# Portable consistency checker — gaps

> **What this is** — where the kernel-free `:consistency` checker ([CONSISTENCY-API](CONSISTENCY-API.md)) reports a model differently from A12's `checkConsistency`: a class it leaves unnamed, or one it names where A12 names another. Each row gives the input, A12's measured class, the kernel law that measured it, and the plan item that settles it. Ids are `DG<n>`, drawn from the chunks declared once in [DEV-API-GAPS](DEV-API-GAPS.md)'s `dg-id-chunks` line, which serves both ledgers.
>
> **Lifecycle.** A gap lives here only while open. When it ships, its row is DELETED in the same commit: the measured behaviour already lives in its [KERNEL-FINDINGS](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md) entry, and the sweep ledger turns the divergence into an agreement. No shipped list, ever.
>
> **Current state.** Since 2026-09-26 the checker's divergences are discovered by the near-miss sweep, and its committed ledger (`adapter/src/test/resources/checker-near-miss/clusters.tsv`) is their list. A row found by hand names its input, the missing structure behind it and the stage rebuild in [CONFORMANCE-PLAN](CONFORMANCE-PLAN.md) that settles it, and is kept here until it becomes a sweep seed.

## 1. Open

- **DG177 — the checker passes a document uniqueness criterion A12 refuses (opened 2026-11-03).** Measured 2026-11-03 on one policy model against `checkConsistency`: a criterion over an optional field, over a missing field, and over no field draws A12's sentence (`… is not set as required.`, `… does not exist in the Document Model.`, `Must specify at least one field.`), and `:consistency` reports nothing on any of the three; a criterion over a required field is clean on both. No `:consistency` source reads the member (`grep -rli uniquenesscriteri consistency/src`, no match). The direction is under-reporting. Settle with a criterion family over [KF671](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf671)'s admission classes, locked by an agreement test.

## 2. Out of scope

- **What a document's evaluation fires.** The checker judges a model statically; runtime behaviour is the interpreter's, and its divergences are [INTERPRETER-GAPS](INTERPRETER-GAPS.md)'.

## 3. Deferred — with re-open triggers

- **DG175 — the JavaScript checker once read `YearFromDate(Time(Hour))` as valid where A12 and the JVM checker draw `MVK_WRONG_DATE_FORMAT_FOR_OP` (opened 2026-11-03).** `TemporalExtractorOperandsTest.aNestedOperationCarriesTheFormatA12RegistersForIt` failed on `:consistency:jsNodeTest` in four consecutive runs on 2026-11-03, a clean `--rerun-tasks` rebuild among them. Its row "the year of a built time" came back `[]`, while the JVM target stayed green throughout. It then passed in the four later runs: three on byte-identical sources, and one with a temporary trace line in `TemporalOperandReading`. Moving the regenerated `OperatorId.SUM_OF_TERMS` to the end of the enum also passed once, and the failing model checked on its own read correctly. Neither establishes a mechanism. A repeat harness at `28432881b` did not reproduce it: 8 forced reruns of the class and 3 forced full `:consistency:jsNodeTest` runs (969 tests each) all passed. The suspected class, an order dependence on a JavaScript identity-hash iteration, is a hypothesis without a run that discriminates it; one rival is ruled out, since no operator shares the spelling `Time` in either language, so the reader's spelling-to-id table cannot answer it two ways. **Re-open trigger:** any recurrence, in any lane. Keep that run's log and test-results tree, which is the reproduction this row lacks; it is settled by a repeated-run harness that reproduces the failure, followed by the one change that removes it. *Deferred 2026-11-04: no run can settle it while it does not recur, and as an open row it would block every release.*
