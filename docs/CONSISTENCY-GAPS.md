# Portable consistency checker — gaps

> **What this is** — where the kernel-free `:consistency` checker ([CONSISTENCY-API](CONSISTENCY-API.md)) reports a model differently from A12's `checkConsistency`: a class it leaves unnamed, or one it names where A12 names another. Each row gives the input, A12's measured class, the kernel law that measured it, and the plan item that settles it. Ids are `DG<n>`, drawn from the chunks declared once in [DEV-API-GAPS](DEV-API-GAPS.md)'s `dg-id-chunks` line, which serves both ledgers.
>
> **Lifecycle.** A gap lives here only while open. When it ships, its row is DELETED in the same commit: the measured behaviour already lives in its [KERNEL-FINDINGS](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md) entry, and the sweep ledger turns the divergence into an agreement. No shipped list, ever.
>
> **Current state.** Since 2026-09-26 the checker's divergences are discovered by the near-miss sweep, and its committed ledger (`adapter/src/test/resources/checker-near-miss/clusters.tsv`) is their list. A row found by hand names its input, the missing structure behind it and the stage rebuild in [CONFORMANCE-PLAN](CONFORMANCE-PLAN.md) that settles it, and is kept here until it becomes a sweep seed.

## 1. Open

- **DG146 — iteration-guard shapes the checker leaves to A12 (opened 2026-10-22, narrowed the same day when the law landed, and again on 2026-10-23 when group error targets and field-keyed selections were measured).** The `ITERATION_GUARD` family reports `MVK_NEG_CONDITION_IN_ITERATION` and `MVK_PARALLEL_ITERATION_INVALID` on the measured shapes ([KF537](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf537), [KF595](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf595), [KF596](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf596), [KF601](KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf601)). It declines, and names the rule in a coverage gap instead (`IterationGuardTest`), where its condition uses a `$` marker, where a rule iterating in parallel reports on a group (measured only on an admitted row, `t8` of `CheckerIterationK04AgreementTest`, whose `theCheckerDeclinesOnlyThePinnedRows` pins every declined row), carries a field-keyed selection, or carries a constant selection that still iterates a level above it, a leaf outside the measured set (a pattern, `RepetitionNotUnique`, a tolerance comparison, the field-and-type `Valid`/`Invalid`, a keyword comparison), a group operand in a field list other than `FieldValuesNotUnique`, or a root group read as a condition, where only a starred reference names the error field while another iterates, where a comparison uses `SumOfProducts`, or where a constant beyond the exact range of a double decides. It also declines a whole connective when any one part is undecided, even where A12's answer is already fixed by the others. Each of those is a silence A12 can break: A12 refuses a root-group condition the checker declines (`CheckerIterationGuardAgreementTest`). Settled shape by shape: a kernel row in that differential, then the leaf in `IterationGuard.kt`.
- **DG156 — a value-in-fields text constant outside an enumeration's domain is left to A12 (opened 2026-10-24).** `NumberOfValueInFields("NOPE" In Tier, Note) > 0` draws `MVK_INVALID_STRING_CONSTANT_FOR_ENUMERATION` where the checker is silent, on a plain field and a type-defined one alike: the type walk in `ValueInFieldsConstantKind.kt` stops at the refusing enumeration as A12 does but names nothing there (`MEMBERSHIP_LEFT_TO_A12` in `CheckerTypeDefinedSettingAgreementTest`; the law row is `rulekit.validate.laws.ValueInFieldsConstantKindLawsTest`). Under-reports; the membership class at the walk's stopping position, measured beside a member constant and a later refusing field, settles it.
